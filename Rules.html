Toolkit Rules

This playthrough includes the Twitch toolkit mod. this allows viewers to interact with the game. Below are some rules relating to the toolkit usage.

Coins

  • Hourly coin rewards
  • More interaction gives more coins
  • Subs receive 5000 bonus coins
  • For every $10 donation, hourly coin rewards will be multiplied by the cumulative dollar amount! ($10x100, $20x100, etc.)
  • Do not beg for coins
  • You may give coins to other viewers

    • *Donation and sub coins may not be immediately given out. Please be patient.*

Redemptions

  • Anything listed can be redeemed
  • Some items may be removed or replaced
  • Good events give positive karma
  • Bad events give negative karma
  • Please do not spam redemptions
  • Please do not Intentionally spawn events with the goal to destroy the colony. Bad events are fine but please do so with moderation.
